# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the MELTYMON AI OS community a welcoming, respectful and constructive experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions regardless of background, experience level, nationality, gender, identity, or personal beliefs.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming constructive feedback and different technical approaches.
- Providing helpful and objective criticism.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Insulting or threatening other participants.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or collaborative work.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional technical community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions and other official community channels.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Inappropriate behavior may result in warnings, removal of content, temporary restrictions or exclusion from project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer through the repository's GitHub profile.

For security vulnerabilities, please follow the separate reporting procedure in `SECURITY.md`.
